Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Better Applications LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("Better Applications," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Prayer First mobile application (the "App"). "Prayer First" is a product of Better Applications LLC. By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

Prayer First is designed to operate entirely on your device. We do not run servers that store your personal data, do not require you to create an account, and do not sell or share your personal information. The only off-device transmission of data we facilitate is your purchase or subscription transaction, which is handled by Apple and not by us.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information stored locally on your device

The following information is created by you while using the App and stored locally on your device (in iOS UserDefaults and a shared App Group container so the App and its extensions can share data):

  • Your first name (entered during onboarding)
  • Prayer history (dates and text of prayers you have completed)
  • Streak, grace-day, and milestone data
  • Reflection entries (mood and connection scores; any text you choose to enter)
  • Lock schedule and Screen Time selections (categories or apps you choose to block)
  • App preferences, settings, and onboarding state

This information stays on your device. We do not have access to it.

2.2 Information Apple shares with us when you purchase a subscription

If you purchase a subscription or in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address, payment card information, or billing address. From Apple, we receive only aggregated and anonymized sales and subscription reports (for example: number of new subscribers, churn, country) and, in connection with our subscription management tooling, a randomized identifier that lets us validate that your subscription is active. We use this information solely to operate and account for the subscription service.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with data brokers.

3. Screen Time & App Blocking (Family Controls)

Prayer First uses Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls (ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity, FamilyControls) frameworks to block apps you choose until you complete your daily prayer. Apple's Screen Time framework is designed so that the App can apply restrictions you have authorized without Apple disclosing the underlying list of installed apps or usage data to us. The selections you make are tokenized by iOS and stored on your device. You can revoke Screen Time authorization at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time. We are not able to read, transmit, or store your Screen Time usage data.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Prayer First schedules local notifications on-device to remind you about your daily prayer. No push-notification server is used and no notification content is transmitted off your device.

5. iCloud Backups

If you have iCloud Backup enabled in your iOS settings, the App's local data may be included in your standard device backup. iCloud backups are managed entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can exclude the App from iCloud Backup at any time in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.

6. Subscriptions & Payments

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple's Privacy Policy governs the information you provide to Apple. We receive only the limited transactional information described in Section 2.2 and do not have access to your payment method.

7. How We Use Information

To the extent we receive any information at all (Section 2.2), we use it only to:

  • Validate that you have an active subscription so the App can unlock subscriber features;
  • Comply with our tax, accounting, and legal obligations;
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the subscription service;
  • Communicate with you if you contact us at the email address below.

We do not use this information for advertising or to build a profile about you.

8. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose limited information only:

  • To service providers that operate on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations (for example, our subscription validation provider, if any). A current list is available on request.
  • To comply with law or respond to lawful requests by public authorities, including to meet national security or law enforcement requirements.
  • To protect rights, property, or safety of Better Applications, our users, or others.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets, in which case we will require the recipient to honor the commitments in this Privacy Policy.

9. Data Retention

Information stored on your device remains there until you delete the App, reset the App's data from within Settings, or remove the data manually. The limited subscription information we receive is retained only as long as required to operate the subscription service and meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations (typically up to seven years for financial records under applicable U.S. law).

10. Security

We rely on Apple's iOS security model to protect data on your device, including device-level encryption and sandboxing of the App's container. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your device passcode and Apple ID credentials secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Prayer First is rated 4+ in the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware of unintentional data collection from a child, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the email below.

We collect this information directly from you and from Apple, and we use it for the business purposes described in Section 7.

Sale / sharing: We do not sell personal information and do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your rights: You have the right to (a) know what personal information we have about you; (b) request deletion; (c) request correction of inaccurate information; (d) limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect any); and (e) be free from retaliation for exercising your rights.
